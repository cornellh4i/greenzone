Untangling the roots of Mongolia's rangeland degradation is a complex challenge — but one of critical importance. With over half of rangelands in Mongolia considered degraded, understanding the dynamics between livestock movement, soil health, and climate can support effective management of this important natural resource. These challenges and more are highlighted in this recently published article in [Earth.Org](https://lnkd.in/ewgq5VNi).

Our model helps to solve these issues by tracking rangelands using earth observation, machine learning and analysis for stakeholders, supporting the way for healthier landscapes and a sustainable rural economy.
